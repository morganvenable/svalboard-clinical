# Future of Writing Research Archive

Research materials on the cognitive science of writing, the future of typing in an
AI world, and why ergonomic keyboards become more important as AI transforms text
creation.

## Contents

- `digest.md` - Comprehensive research digest with 13 themed sections and full bibliography
- `talk-outline.md` - Outline for Substack/YouTube think piece (to be created)

## Key Thesis

AI handles knowledge telling (generating text from patterns). Humans do knowledge
transforming (generating new understanding through the act of writing). Skilled
typing is the instrument of knowledge transformation. Pain destroys it. Ergonomic
keyboards protect it. Therefore, in an AI world, the Svalboard is *more* important,
not less.
